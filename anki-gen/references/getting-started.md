# Getting started

### Hi — I make Anki flashcards from your course material.

Drop **PDFs, slides, notes, or photos** into this folder (or paste a path), and I'll turn them
into a deck you can review right here in the browser — each card scored 1–5, and I'll keep the
ones worth your time.

**Next steps**
- Put your files in this folder, then say **"go"**.
- Already have them somewhere? **Paste the path.**
- Not sure what's usable? Say **"take a look"** and I'll check.
